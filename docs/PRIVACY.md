# PhotoGate privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

PhotoGate helps you decide which new photos and videos on your phone get synced to your cloud photo service.
This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Summary

**PhotoGate does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Nothing leaves your phone through PhotoGate.**
The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## What PhotoGate accesses, and why

| Access | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 11–12) | To notice new photos in the folders you choose and show them for review | On your phone |
| Media management (optional, Android 12+) | To move photos you upload into the PhotoGate folders and delete photos you bin or delete, without a confirmation each time | On your phone |
| Notifications | To tell you about new photos to review | On your phone |

PhotoGate changes photos only when you ask it to: **Upload** moves a photo into `DCIM/PhotoGate/<category>` on
your phone and writes the category into the photo's description. **Bin** and **Delete now** delete the photo from
your phone.

## Syncing to the cloud

PhotoGate itself never uploads anything. If you set up your gallery app (for example Samsung Gallery with Google
Photos sync) to sync the PhotoGate folders, that app does the uploading, under its own privacy policy.

## Data stored by the app

Your settings (chosen folders, categories, timers) and the list of photos waiting for a decision are stored only in
the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling PhotoGate removes them. Your photos themselves are not
affected by uninstalling.

## Children

PhotoGate is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
